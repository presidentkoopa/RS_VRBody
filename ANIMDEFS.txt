// ==========================================================================
// THE MIRROR'S SURFACE.
//
// A camera texture: the scene rendered from a viewpoint actor, straight onto a
// model's skin. That is the whole trick behind being able to LOOK AT YOURSELF.
//
// Why a mirror and not a third-person camera: a chase camera in VR rides your
// view direction, so turning your head swings the camera through an arc instead
// of pivoting at your neck. Your inner ear says "I turned my head" and your eyes
// say "I swung sideways", and that is what makes people ill. A mirror moves
// NOTHING. Your head still drives your camera; you walk up to a panel and look
// at it, exactly as you would in a room. See the standing rule beside the recoil
// work: nothing we compute may move the camera.
//
// 512 square: this renders the whole scene again, so it is an on-demand toy for
// checking the body, never something left switched on.
// ==========================================================================
cameratexture RSMIRROR 512 512
	fit 512 512
